## Auth0 Documentation

### Organizations
Auth0 Organizations let you represent your business customers as isolated tenants, each with
their own members, connections, and branding.

- [Organizations overview](https://auth0.com/docs/manage-users/organizations)
- [Login flows for organizations](https://auth0.com/docs/manage-users/organizations/login-flows-for-organizations)
- [Identifier First / Home Realm Discovery](https://auth0.com/docs/authenticate/login/auth0-universal-login/identifier-first)

### Enterprise Connections
Enterprise connections allow your customers' employees to log in using their existing
corporate identity provider (SAML, OIDC, Entra ID, Okta, Google Workspace, etc.).

- [Enterprise connections overview](https://auth0.com/docs/authenticate/enterprise-connections)
- [SAML identity providers](https://auth0.com/docs/authenticate/identity-providers/enterprise-identity-providers/saml)
- [OIDC identity providers](https://auth0.com/docs/authenticate/identity-providers/enterprise-identity-providers/oidc)

### Self-Service SSO (SSEC)
Self-Service Enterprise Configuration lets you delegate SSO setup to your enterprise customers
via a one-time setup link, so they configure their own IdP without your involvement.

- [Self-Service Enterprise Configuration overview](https://auth0.com/docs/authenticate/enterprise-connections/self-service-enterprise-configuration)
- [Management API: self-service-profiles](https://auth0.com/docs/api/management/v2)
